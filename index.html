How to Play
  1. A five-letter word appears at the top of the screen, shown as colorful bubbles.
  2. Tap those letters to spell a shorter word, then tap the arrow button to submit your guess.
  3. If your guess matches one of the hidden words, its letters appear in the white boxes below.
  4. If your guess is a real word but not one of the hidden ones, it's added to your word bank instead.
  5. Stuck? Tap the 💡 next to any hidden word for a free letter hint, as many times as you like.
  6. Find every hidden word to win!
💡 Tip: letters can be used more than once! For example, if the main word has one "O", you can still spell a word that uses "O" twice.
